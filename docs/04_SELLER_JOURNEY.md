# 04 · Seller Journey: Master Process

The 60 steps below are the **internal process specification**. Homeowners never see them as 60 steps. They see **6 stages**, a daily action center, and only what applies to their transaction.

## Customer-facing stages
| Stage | Seller sees | Internal steps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Explore & Get Started | Home value, net proceeds, service options, property intake, ownership, agreements | 1–11 |
| 2. Prepare Your Home | Pricing, disclosures, preparation, photography, listing content, professional review | 12–30 |
| 3. Manage Your Listing | MLS status, showing requests, appointments, feedback, activity reports | 31–40 |
| 4. Review Offers | Offer summaries, comparisons, broker advice, counteroffers, acceptance | 41–45 |
| 5. Manage Escrow & Closing | Inspections, financing, contingencies, title, closing costs, documents, keys | 46–58 |
| 6. Your Completed Sale | Closing summary, records, follow-up, next-home planning | 59–60 |

The Fairward Assistant works across every stage, and the licensed broker sees the same records.

## The 60 steps (condensed)
In the original 12 phases. **P** = platform support; **L** = licensed professional's role.

**Phase 1: Deciding to sell**
1. **Exploring.** P: self-assessment, calculator, process overview. L: optional conversation.
2. **Equity and net proceeds.** P: preliminary net sheet labeled as an estimate. L: refines it before listing.
3. **Financial complications.** P: checklist (liens, HELOC, solar lease/PPA, PACE, Mello-Roos, HOA delinquency, taxes). L: advice and referrals.
4. **Tax and life events.** P: neutral education and "questions for your tax pro". L: refers out.
5. **Next home.** P: sell-first / buy-first / rent-back planner. L: sequencing advice.
6. **Choosing how to sell.** P: comparison, scope and transparent pricing.

**Phase 2: Getting started**
7. **Account and identity verification**, plus two-factor sign-in.
8. **Ownership and signing authority.** Vesting check against county records; co-sellers, trusts and powers of attorney flagged.
9. **Property profile.** Public records pre-filled for the seller to correct; the "what makes this home special" interview.
10. **Goals and constraints.**
11. **Service scope and listing agreement.** Plain-language clauses, e-sign, broker countersign.

**Phase 3: Pricing**
12. **Market research.** P organizes the data. L prepares the CMA.
13. **List price.** L recommends; the seller decides; the decision is recorded.
14. **Price review checkpoints.** No automated price-cut prompts.

**Phase 4: Preparing the home** (reduced to essentials for v1)
15. Condition review / pre-listing inspection.
16. Repairs, with the verified upgrade ledger.
17. Point-of-sale safety items (smoke/CO detectors, water heater strapping, local rules).
18. Declutter, clean and stage.
19. Curb appeal.
20. Safety, valuables and the privacy sweep.

**Phase 5: Disclosures and documents**
21. **Rules-based disclosure checklist**, conditional on property type, age, ownership, transaction type and agreements.
22. HOA documents.
23. Document vault (permits, warranties, solar, utilities).
24. Early preliminary title report.

**Phase 6: Marketing (Marketing Studio; see doc 08)**
25. Photography and video.
26. Floor plan (draft measurements) and tour.
27. Listing copy from verified facts, plus the fair housing language check.
28. Labeled virtual staging, with originals preserved.
29. Campaign assets.
30. Marketing approval trail.

**Phase 7: Going live**
31. MLS input (broker-controlled).
32. Launch timing per the MLS rules that actually apply.
33. Syndication and property page.
34. Buyer-broker compensation decision (separate workflow, not an MLS field).

**Phase 8: Showings**
35. Showing setup and access rules.
36. Requests (accept / decline / suggest a time).
37. Open houses.
38. Feedback themes and weekly report.
39. Buyer questions become the property FAQ.
40. Security and access logs. **Buyers without an agent: ID verification, accompanied showings, no lockbox access.**

**Phase 9: Offers**
41. Offer inbox and deadlines.
42. Side-by-side comparison with illustrative net.
43. Response (accept / counter / decline / multiple counters). L advises; the seller decides.
44. Back-up offers.
45. Acceptance, which generates the deadline calendar.

**Phase 10: Escrow**
46. Open escrow; wire-fraud protections.
47. Disclosure delivery and acknowledgment.
48. Inspections.
49. Repair requests.
50. Appraisal.
51. Buyer financing status.
52. Contingency removals.
53. Title, payoffs, FIRPTA and California withholding, solar/PACE transfers.
54. Cancellation (now a full exception workflow; see below).

**Phase 11: Closing**
55. Move plan.
56. Final walkthrough.
57. Closing statement review and signing.
58. Recording, possession and keys.

**Phase 12: After closing**
59. **Records.** Secure, exportable access for a defined period. Brokerage retention of at least 3 years (longer under a litigation hold) is a separate obligation.
60. **Wrap-up.** Takedown requests for **Fairward-controlled** pages, the home manual for the buyer, survey, referrals.

**Special paths:** trust, estate or probate · divorce · tenant-occupied · short sale · solar, ADU or unpermitted work · relocation · seniors and accessibility · expired or withdrawn listings.

## System gates and rules
- **Ready for MLS gate.** A formal status, not a percentage. All of these must be complete:
  - listing agreement signed
  - ownership and authority verified
  - material facts verified
  - disclosures addressed
  - photos and copy approved
  - marketing and compensation instructions documented
  - **broker authorization** (the final, non-delegable step)

  The gate **re-closes** on material changes: price, photos, copy or an altered image.
- **Deadline engine.** Deadlines are calculated from the actual signed terms, calendar rules and amendments. Each date is *Calculated* until it's *Broker-confirmed*, and amendments reset affected dates to Calculated.
- **Status provenance everywhere.** Every figure and fact is marked confirmed, estimated or missing; verified or unverified.
- **Permissions per action.** *View / comment / approve / sign*, with signing authority per document type, verified once and enforced by the system.
- **Never let AI** fill in property-condition answers from documents. The seller confirms facts; the broker reviews.

## Exception workflows (structured, not ad-hoc AI chat)
Buyer financing failure · missed deadline · inspection dispute · low appraisal · buyer or seller cancellation · back-up activation · return to market · expired or withdrawn listing.

Each one has a playbook with steps, an owner per step, deadlines and the required broker recommendation. *(The low-appraisal playbook is prototyped in the broker console.)*

## Platform threads across all stages
1. **Personalized daily action center:** needs you / waiting on others / coming next. Each item shows its owner, deadline, status and whether broker approval is needed.
2. **Live net-proceeds dashboard:** confirmed, estimated and missing figures.
3. **Shared decision-maker access:** separate identities; viewing ≠ approving ≠ signing.
4. **Transaction health monitoring:** overdue tasks, missing documents, approaching deadlines, stalled communication. No predictions about whether the sale will close.
5. **Internal time and cost tracking** on every file.
