# 08 · Marketing Studio: Concept

A differentiator that helps sellers capture their home, turn the materials into a compelling presentation, and understand buyer interest, with licensed review before anything goes public.

## Core capabilities
| Feature | Seller experience | Approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guided home capture | Room-by-room shot list with framing and lighting tips | Mobile-friendly web first; quality checks later |
| Floor-plan scanning | Walk through rooms to generate a draft layout | Future native iOS app with Apple RoomPlan (LiDAR); web displays the results. **Scans are draft measurements, never advertised as authoritative square footage** |
| Interactive floor plans | Buyers tap a room to see photos, video and verified features | Own viewer; works with uploaded plans before scanning exists |
| Self-guided tours | "Explore the kitchen", "See outdoor spaces", full walkthrough | Linked photos and video chapters first; Matterport SDK later; support external captures (e.g. Zillow 3D Home) instead of building a scanning engine |
| Video production assistant | Shot list, suggested narration, sequence | Guided scripts and uploads first; captioning and trimming later |
| Property storytelling | Listing copy, room descriptions, brochure, emails | AI drafts **from verified facts only**, seller approval, licensed review |
| Seller video guides | Prep, staging, showing readiness | Guide library tied to tasks |
| Campaign builder | Property page, QR flyer, email, social assets from one library | Templates first; integrations later |
| Buyer engagement insights | Tour visits, popular rooms, video completion, showing requests | Privacy-conscious, aggregate; **interest is never presented as offers or a price prediction** |

## Distinctly Fairward additions
- **Light planner:** uses home orientation and sun times to suggest when to shoot each room.
- **Instant shot review:** blur, horizon, darkness, lights off, clutter.
- **Privacy sweep:** family photos, names, documents, plates, security panels, medication.
- **Broker markup:** the broker circles part of a photo with "reshoot"; it becomes a task.
- **Partial photographer booking:** book a pro for specific rooms only.
- **"What makes this home special?" interview:** facts are verified before use.
- **Fact sheet with sources:** every claim links to evidence; unverified claims stay out of public materials.
- **Verified upgrade ledger:** permits and receipts; also feeds the disclosures.
- **Running-cost profile** (opt-in): utility bills, solar production, HOA dues.
- **Neighborhood facts from public data only.** No school-quality or demographic claims.
- **Fair housing language check** on all drafts.
- **Translated property pages**, reviewed before publishing (Spanish, Chinese, Vietnamese, Hmong, Russian/Ukrainian).
- **Accessibility details:** step-free entry, door widths, alt text, audio-described tour.
- **Original vs. virtually staged toggle:** clearly labeled, originals preserved (California AB 723; MLS rules).
- **Property potential overlays** (renovation/ADU): separately labeled, with feasibility status.
- **Tour-to-question workflow:** answers become the property FAQ once seller-approved.
- **"Will my furniture fit?":** buyers place furniture on the draft floor plan, labeled approximate.
- **Open-house mode:** QR check-in (opt-in), room cards on phones, exit feedback.
- **Weekly seller report** and feedback themes with factual responses.
- **Cover-photo testing.**
- **Print kit:** QR yard-sign rider, neighbor postcards.
- **Home manual for the buyer at closing**, with the seller's permission.
- **Marketing readiness score:** completeness and review status only; never predicts price.

## Compliance built in
- **Approval trail:** Draft → Seller-approved → Broker-approved → Published, with an audit log.
- **Automatic disclosures:** brokerage name and license number on every advertisement.
- **MLS photo rules checks.**
- **Labeled altered images** linked to their originals.
- **Publication timing guardrails** based on the MLS rules that actually apply (no hard-coded timeline).
- **Media rights tracking:** photographer licenses and consent for any people shown.
- **Takedown scope:** requests apply to Fairward-controlled pages; removal from every third-party portal can't be guaranteed.
