# 01 · Vision & Business Model

## What Fairward is
A California real estate brokerage built around four things:
- **Lower fees.** A proposed flat listing fee ($2,995, *Proposed Pilot Pricing*) instead of a percentage.
- **Intelligent technology.** Guided workflows, organization, reminders, transaction visibility and an AI assistant for education.
- **Consumer control.** Sellers are directly involved and see everything about their sale.
- **Licensed professional oversight.** Representation, advice, negotiation and supervision wherever professional judgment is required.

Fairward is **not** an FSBO listing service and **not** a conventional brokerage with a chatbot attached. AI never independently performs licensed activities and is never presented as replacing licensed professionals.

## Who it serves
- **Primary: sellers.** Homeowners considering FSBO, preparing to list, or looking for an alternative to traditional fees. The main conversion is a qualified seller inquiry.
- **Secondary: buyers.** Self-directed buyers who value transparency (a buyer interest list today; buyer representation in development). The secondary conversion is a buyer interest submission.

## Market
Sacramento first, then other California markets, then possibly nationwide.

## Emotional outcome
> "This feels like a much smarter way to sell my home, and I can understand exactly what I'm getting."

## Distinguishing qualities
More consumer control · lower potential brokerage expenses · clear transaction information · intelligent digital workflows · licensed professional oversight.

## Economics (to be validated)
- **Revenue:** the $2,995 flat listing fee is proposed. Payment timing is **not** confirmed; the site never says "upfront" or "at closing".
- **Core risk:** professional labor hours per file. Difficult cases (exceptions, special situations, accompanied showings for buyers without an agent) consume broker time that a flat fee may not cover.
- **Instrumentation:** every broker review, support interaction, showing and exception task is time-logged (see the broker console's *Time & economics* screen).
- **Decisions this informs:**
  - included hours or showings in the service terms
  - add-on pricing for exceptions or special paths (probate, trust, short sale)
  - routing files to salespersons versus brokers
  - break-even hours at a loaded hourly cost

## Business model guardrails
- All brokerage fees are negotiable and not set by law; comparisons are always illustrative.
- No guaranteed savings, results, rebates or "no hidden costs" claims.
- No fabricated reviews, statistics, team members or credentials.
- Buyer-broker compensation is a separate seller decision recorded in writing; it is never an MLS field.

## Team model
Today there is **one designated broker** (the founder). The platform is designed to add:
- **Associate brokers:** can approve most items.
- **Salespersons (agents):** prepare work; a supervising broker approves it.
- **Transaction coordinators (unlicensed):** scheduling and admin only.
- **Multiple offices:** later.
